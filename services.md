## 💎 The Rani Core: Detail-Clean Rotation System®
Our signature cleaning methodology ensures every part of your property receives a deep clean on a consistent, predictable schedule.

### Step 1: Baseline Stabilization (Visits 1 & 2)
We begin with two intensive sessions to bring your property to our "Gold Standard."
- **Phase A**: Deep clean of Kitchen and Bathrooms + meticulous standard clean of all other living/sleeping areas.
- **Phase B**: Deep clean of Sleeping and Living areas + meticulous maintenance of Kitchen and Bathrooms.

### Step 2: The Continuous Detail Cycle
Once stabilized, your property enters a 4-zone rotation. On every visit, one zone receives an "Intensive Detail" while the others receive "Elite Maintenance."
- **Zone 1: The Culinary Suite (Kitchen)**
- **Zone 2: The Sanitary Suites (Bathrooms)**
- **Zone 3: The Rest Quarters (Bedrooms)**
- **Zone 4: The Social Spaces (Living/Dining/Entry)**

## 📝 Section III: Exhaustive Room-by-Room Checklists

### 🍳 The Kitchen (Intensive Detail)
- [ ] **Appliances**: Clean/polish range hood (inside/out), stove drip pans/glass top, inside microwave, and all stainless steel.
- [ ] **Cabinets**: Hand-wipe all cabinet fronts and hardware (degreasing treatment).
- [ ] **Surfaces**: Sanitize all countertops and backsplashes (stone-specific cleaners used).
- [ ] **Detail**: Clean under/behind appliances (where accessible), hand-wipe baseboards and window sills.

### 🚿 The Bathroom (Intensive Detail)
- [ ] **Sanitation**: Scrub and disinfect tubs, showers, tile walls, and toilets (base/behind/bottom).
- [ ] **Polishing**: Chrome fixtures, luxury faucets, and mirrors polished to a streak-free shine.
- [ ] **Tile Care**: Scrub grout lines and remove all lime/calcium buildup.
- [ ] **Floors**: Hand-wipe floor edges, baseboards, and bathroom door frames.

### 🛌 Sleeping & Living Areas (Intensive Detail)
- [ ] **Dusting**: Hand-dust all horizontal surfaces including picture frames, lamps, and knickknacks.
- [ ] **Upholstery**: Vacuum all upholstered furniture including under and between cushions.
- [ ] **Entrances**: Clean sliding glass doors (tracks included) and front entry thresholds.
- [ ] **Vents/Fans**: Dust all ceiling fans and air vents (within step-ladder reach).
